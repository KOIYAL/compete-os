# Deliverable gates

- Story can be understood without the presenter.
- Every judging claim has visible evidence.
- Live demo completes with fixed, non-sensitive data.
- Offline fallback is stored locally and opens.
- Deck contains no unsupported market, impact, or implementation claim.
- Pitch fits the allotted time in two rehearsals.
- Submission checklist matches the current organizer form.
- Every public URL is tested from a fresh session.
