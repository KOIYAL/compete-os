# Intake checklist

- Organizer and official source
- Event or application type
- Eligibility and team rules
- Start, freeze, rehearsal, submission, and presentation times
- Required technology or format
- Deliverables and submission channels
- Judging criteria and weights
- Prior-work, open-source, AI-tool, and template rules
- Intellectual-property and publicity terms
- Privacy, confidentiality, and data restrictions
- License and attribution requirements
- Questions requiring organizer confirmation
